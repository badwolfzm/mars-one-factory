# Mars One Factory: The Plan in Plain English

**The Mission**  
Build one single orbital factory (a converted Starship tanker) that can be switched on in under 90 days and start mass-producing ultra-cheap ($100–150 per person), backpack-sized, fully inflatable life-support kits.  
Each kit feeds, waters, and oxygenates 25 humans forever on Mars using nothing but sunlight, Martian CO₂, and subsurface ice after the first 60 sols.

Every uncrewed cargo Starship starting in 2031 carries thousands of these rolled-up kits.  
By the time the first humans arrive (2037–2040) step off the first crewed ship, there are already enough kits on the surface to keep hundreds of thousands of people alive indefinitely — even if every future ship is lost and Earth goes silent forever.

Total cost to make 1,000,000 humans immortal on Mars: less than the price of three orbital refueling flights.

This is not sci-fi.  
It is the exact same system that makes Israel food-secure forever, just pointed at Mars instead of missiles.

### How It Actually Works

1. **The Kit Itself** (already solvable with 2025 technology)
   - Same Chinese drop-stitch TPU fabric that flew on Starship IFT-4/5 (pool-toy material, but radiation-resistant and vacuum-qualified).
   Rolls up to the size of an IKEA bag, weighs <35 kg for 25 people.
   Inflates in <5 minutes with a rover compressor and Martian CO₂.
   72 m² of NFT channels + duckweed + black soldier fly larvae → 3,000 kcal/day per person, full protein, indefinitely.
   Water loop closed >98 % using condensate from air and urine.
   O₂ excess from electrolysis of harvested water + CO₂ scrubbing.
   Only moving part: one 15 W solar-powered air blower. Everything else is gravity-fed.
   Atmospheric water generators built from scrap Starship chiller cores (same as dead AC units in Israel version).

2. **The Factory**
   Take one regular Starship tanker in LEO.
   During a normal refueling stop, a 4-person crew spends 30–45 days installing 40 heat-sealing stations inside the payload bay.
   Load 100,000 m² of TPU film and the sealing machines on Earth — total extra mass <15 tons.
   While the ship coasts to Mars (7–8 months), the factory runs 24/7 in zero-g and produces ~5,000 complete kits.
   Kits are vacuum-baked and folded, then landed with the ship.

   Repeat every launch window → 50,000-person life-support capacity added every 26 months, forever.

3. **Pre-Positioning = Invincibility**
   From 2031 onward, every single uncrewed cargo Starship carries at least 2,000 kits whether it’s delivering anything else or not.
   By 2040, before the million-person city is even planned, there are already enough kits on the ground to keep everyone alive forever if Earth never sends another gram.

4. **Economics That Actually Work**
   The entire program pays for itself in peacetime by selling the digital IP (CAD files, SOPs, training) on Earth to governments, preppers, lunar bases, disaster zones.
   Same model as the Israel version: export the knowledge, fund the hardware.
